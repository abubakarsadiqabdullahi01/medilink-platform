# Data inventory and retention (proposed)

**PROPOSED. Not signed off by counsel or the data protection officer, and none of the lawful bases below has been confirmed by either.**

Generated from `src/modules/audit-compliance/domain/data-inventory.ts` by `pnpm tsx scripts/render-data-inventory.ts`. Do not edit this file by hand: change that one, and a test fails until this is regenerated.

It says, for each kind of data the platform keeps: what it holds, why, on what basis, who answers for it, how long it is kept, and what takes it out of the system. A category marked "Proposed, not enforced" has a shorter period the team would suggest that nothing carries out yet.

## What is deleted automatically

A scheduled job (`retention.purge_sweep`, daily) deletes only what no obligation could want kept. Each run that deletes anything writes one audit row with counts and nothing about a person.

| Data | Deleted after | Counted from |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One-time codes, used or not | 7 days | it was issued. A code is valid for ten minutes; the budget on how many may be sent looks back one hour. |
| Sessions that have ended | 30 days | it expired or was signed out. A live session is never touched. |
| The log of sign-in attempts, with the number or identifier typed and the network address | 90 days | the attempt. The lockout looks back minutes, and a disputed lockout is raised within weeks. |
| Entries in the background queue that were delivered | 30 days | delivery. Entries that failed or gave up are never purged: somebody still has to look at them. |
| Invitations to work for a facility, with the phone number invited | 90 days | it ended: answered, cancelled or run out. A pending one is never touched. The facts of it stay in the audit trail, which holds the role and the dates and never the number. |
| Files requested for upload that were never confirmed (the record of the request stays, marked deleted) | 7 days | the request. The link to upload expires within minutes; a week is long enough for a form left half filled to be finished. |

## Categories

### Accounts

- **Tables:** `identity.users`, `identity.devices`
- **Holds:** Full name, phone number, email address, a hash of the password, account status, sign-in times; device labels and push tokens.
- **Purpose:** To run the account and sign the person in.
- **Proposed basis:** Contract: providing the service the person signed up for.
- **Owner:** Operations
- **Retention today:** For the life of the account. A request to erase replaces the name, number and email, deletes devices and closes the account.
- **What removes it:** Deleted or replaced when the person asks to be erased

### Roles and who holds them

- **Tables:** `identity.roles`, `identity.permissions`, `identity.role_permissions`, `identity.user_roles`
- **Holds:** Which person holds which role over which hospital or area, who granted it and when. The role catalogue itself is not about a person.
- **Purpose:** To decide what each person may do, and to answer who could do what on a given day.
- **Proposed basis:** Legitimate interest (security) and the accountability the platform owes its users.
- **Owner:** Platform engineering
- **Retention today:** Kept. A revoked grant stays, stamped, so past access can be reconstructed. Erasure revokes a person's grants and keeps the rows.
- **What removes it:** Kept; nothing deletes it

### Invitations to work for a facility

- **Tables:** `identity.staff_invitations`
- **Holds:** The phone number a facility invited, which may belong to somebody who has not registered; the role offered, who sent it, when it runs out and how it was answered; whether the invitation text reached the number and, if not, why.
- **Purpose:** To let a facility add a colleague with their agreement, and to answer who invited whom and what became of it.
- **Proposed basis:** Legitimate interest: a facility asked to add a colleague and the person is told by one text and may ignore it. Counsel to confirm for the number of somebody who never registers.
- **Owner:** Operations
- **Retention today:** Deleted by the purge job (see below) once the invitation has ended. Erasure of the person it was for ends a waiting one at once and removes the number. The audit trail keeps the role and the dates and never the number.
- **What removes it:** Deleted by a scheduled job (90 days)

### One-time codes

- **Tables:** `identity.otp_codes`
- **Holds:** A hash of each code, what it was for, and the network address it was requested from.
- **Purpose:** To prove somebody controls a phone number, and to limit how many are sent.
- **Proposed basis:** Legitimate interest (security).
- **Owner:** Platform engineering
- **Retention today:** Deleted by the purge job (see below). Erasure deletes a person's earlier.
- **What removes it:** Deleted by a scheduled job (7 days)

- **Purpose:** To keep a person signed in, and to let them see and end their sessions.
- **Proposed basis:** Contract: providing the service.
- **Owner:** Platform engineering
- **Retention today:** Deleted by the purge job (see below). Erasure deletes a person's earlier.
- **What removes it:** Deleted by a scheduled job (30 days)

- **Purpose:** To slow down guessing, and to investigate an account somebody says was attacked or wrongly locked.
- **Proposed basis:** Legitimate interest (security).
- **Owner:** Platform engineering
- **Retention today:** Deleted by the purge job (see below). Erasure deletes a person's earlier.
- **What removes it:** Deleted by a scheduled job (90 days)

### Practitioner profile, registrations and screening

- **Tables:** `practitioners.practitioner_profiles`, `practitioners.professional_registrations`, `practitioners.screening_results`, `practitioners.identity_verifications`
- **Holds:** Profession, specialties, bio, photograph, home area; the register folio number and licence dates; the outcome of screening against the sanctions lists; a hash of the national identity number.
- **Purpose:** To decide whether a person may be offered work, and to show a hospital who is covering a shift.
- **Proposed basis:** Contract, and the obligation to confirm a regulated professional is registered.
- **Owner:** Compliance
- **Retention today:** For the life of the account. Erasure replaces the profile and folio number, deletes the identity-number hash, and clears screening notes.
- **What removes it:** Deleted or replaced when the person asks to be erased

### Credential submissions

- **Tables:** `credentials.credential_submissions`
- **Holds:** What a practitioner submitted as evidence, the reviewer's decision and notes, the expiry date, and the reference to the stored document.
- **Purpose:** To verify qualifications and licences before a person works.
- **Proposed basis:** Contract, and the obligation to verify a regulated professional.
- **Owner:** Compliance
- **Retention today:** For the life of the account. Erasure clears the reviewer's notes and deletes the stored documents.
- **What removes it:** Deleted or replaced when the person asks to be erased
- **Proposed, not enforced:** Proposed: the stored document of a submission that was rejected is deleted twelve months after the rejection, whether or not the person ever asks.

### Uploaded files

- **Tables:** `storage.stored_objects`
- **Holds:** A record of each file in storage (credential documents, licences, photographs, dispute evidence, incident attachments, timesheet attachments): its type, size, owner and key. The files themselves are in the bucket.
- **Purpose:** To keep what people uploaded as evidence, and to find it again.
- **Proposed basis:** Contract, and the purpose of the thing each file is attached to.
- **Owner:** Compliance
- **Retention today:** With the record each file is attached to. Erasure deletes a person's files from storage. An upload nobody finished is deleted by the purge job.
- **What removes it:** Deleted or replaced when the person asks to be erased (7 days)

### Facilities

- **Tables:** `facilities.facilities`
- **Holds:** Name, registration reference (CAC, ministry or other), address, contact number and email, the licence file, the verification decision and what was checked.
- **Purpose:** To verify a hospital before it can request staff, and to contact it.
- **Proposed basis:** Contract.
- **Owner:** Operations
- **Retention today:** For as long as the facility is on the platform, and after that for as long as its invoices must be kept.
- **What removes it:** Kept; nothing deletes it

### Shift requests, offers and assignments

- **Tables:** `service_requests.service_requests`, `matching.invitations`, `matching.assignments`, `matching.assignment_status_history`, `matching.match_runs`, `matching.match_candidates`
- **Holds:** The shift a hospital asked for, who was offered it, who accepted, how each status changed and why; how every candidate was scored and ranked on a search.
- **Purpose:** To arrange shifts and to answer afterwards who covered a ward and how they came to.
- **Proposed basis:** Contract.
- **Owner:** Operations
- **Retention today:** Kept: it is the hospital's record of who covered a ward and the basis of what was paid. Erasure removes a decline reason and leaves the rest.
- **What removes it:** Kept; nothing deletes it
- **Proposed, not enforced:** Proposed: the scores and rankings of a search (match runs and candidates) are deleted twelve months after the search, once a booking is settled.

- **Retention today:** For the life of the account. Erasure deletes it.

- **Proposed basis:** Contract.

- **Holds:** What was invoiced and paid, who was owed what and when it was released, the rate agreed. Records that money moved, not who the person is.
- **Purpose:** To bill hospitals and pay practitioners, and to account for it.
- **Proposed basis:** Legal obligation (tax and accounting) and contract.
- **Owner:** Finance
- **Retention today:** Kept, and never deleted today. The legal period is to be confirmed by counsel and an accountant.
- **What removes it:** Kept; nothing deletes it

### Payout bank accounts

- **Tables:** `billing.payout_accounts`
- **Holds:** The bank, the name the account is held under, its last four digits, and the whole number sealed under a key kept outside the database; a keyed hash of the number so one account given twice is found; who verified it and any reason given.
- **Purpose:** To pay a practitioner what they are owed, to the right account.
- **Proposed basis:** Contract: paying for the work.
- **Owner:** Finance
- **Retention today:** For as long as the account is the practitioner's, and an account they replace is kept for the audit of what was paid where. Erasure deletes every one. The whole number is opened only to show finance where to transfer, and each time is audited.
- **What removes it:** Deleted or replaced when the person asks to be erased

### Messages sent

- **Tables:** `notifications.messages`
- **Holds:** Every text or notice sent: who to (masked), what it said, when, which gateway took it and the id that gateway gave it, and whether it arrived (the gateway's reported status and a short reason code; never the number). The wording can mention a shift, an amount or a decision.
- **Purpose:** To avoid telling a person the same thing twice, and to show what they were told.
- **Proposed basis:** Contract, and legitimate interest.
- **Owner:** Operations
- **Retention today:** Kept. Erasure deletes a person's messages.
- **What removes it:** Deleted or replaced when the person asks to be erased
- **Proposed, not enforced:** Proposed: messages are deleted twelve months after they were sent.

### Audit trail

- **Tables:** `audit.audit_log`, `audit.audit_digests`
- **Holds:** Who did what to what, and when: identifiers, the reason given and the before and after of the change. Names, email addresses and phone numbers are not written into it. With it, for each day that is over, how many rows the day held and a hash of them, which says nothing about anybody.
- **Purpose:** Accountability for every change the platform makes.
- **Proposed basis:** Legal obligation and legitimate interest.
- **Owner:** Data protection officer (not yet appointed)
- **Retention today:** Kept, and not deleted: the person is told so before they ask. Rows written before the privacy change (AUD-02) may still hold what that change now keeps out.
- **What removes it:** Kept; nothing deletes it

### Security events

- **Purpose:** Evidence of who looked at what, for a record that is read but not changed.
- **Proposed basis:** Legitimate interest (security).
- **Owner:** Platform engineering
- **Retention today:** Kept. Erasure removes the person from the events that name them.
- **What removes it:** Deleted or replaced when the person asks to be erased
- **Proposed, not enforced:** Proposed: events are deleted twenty-four months after they happened.

- **Owner:** Platform engineering
